## What we do

**Courses.** Programming, Python, machine learning, LLMs and AI automation, taught in
Bulgarian. Live online, plus hands-on hackathons in the mountains. Technical terms stay in
English, because that is what you will meet in the documentation.

**IT and AI services.** Automation and custom tools for individuals and small businesses —
the kind of work that is usually quoted as an enterprise project and does not have to be.
Remote, and in person around Blagoevgrad and Sandanski.

**Research.** Applied AI/ML, with a long-standing interest in symbolic AI, ontologies and
knowledge graphs.

## What's here

Public tools and demonstrations, most of them built with AI agents and documented so you can
see exactly how they were made: local-first AI tools that keep your data on your machine,
experiments with models on Bulgarian text, ready-to-use templates for small businesses, and
tooling for working with coding agents.

The pinned repositories below are a good place to start. The full list, with the story behind
each project, is in the [project portfolio](https://sciscend.com/ivapopova/projects/).

## Founder

[SciScend](https://sciscend.com/) was founded by **Iva Popova** - trainer in Python, machine
learning and front-end development since 2015, software engineer since 2002, and a former
assistant professor of AI and programming languages at the Technical University of Sofia.
Doctoral research in AI systems: semantic retrieval, ontologies and NLP.
