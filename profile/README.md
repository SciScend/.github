## What we do

**Courses.** Programming, Python, machine learning, LLMs and AI automation, taught in
Bulgarian. Live online, plus hands-on hackathons in the mountains. Technical terms stay in
English, because that is what you will meet in the documentation.

**IT and AI services.** Automation and custom tools for individuals and small businesses —
the kind of work that is usually quoted as an enterprise project and does not have to be.
Remote, and in person around Blagoevgrad and Sandanski.

**Research.** Applied AI/ML, with a long-standing interest in symbolic AI, ontologies and
knowledge graphs.

## What's here

Public tools and demonstrations, most of them built with AI agents and documented so you can
see exactly how they were made.

- **[meme-collagen](https://github.com/SciScend/meme-collagen)** — a browser meme and collage
  editor with no dependencies, no build step and no server.
  [Try it](https://sciscend.github.io/meme-collagen/). Written by an AI agent from four
  prompts in a single session; the README shows the numbers.

## Who

SciScend is run by **Iva Popova** — trainer in Python, machine learning and front-end
development since 2015, software engineer since 2002, and a former assistant professor of AI
and programming languages at the Technical University of Sofia. Doctoral research in AI
systems: semantic retrieval, ontologies and NLP.

The company is deliberately one person plus a lot of automation. The repositories here are
part of the argument that this is now enough.
